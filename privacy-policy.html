Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Radha Madhav Caterers ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you visit radhamadhavcaterers.in (the "website") or contact us to enquire about or book our catering services. By using our website or submitting the booking form, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

Radha Madhav Caterers is a pure-vegetarian catering business based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. For any privacy-related questions or requests, our contact details are provided at the end of this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill in our booking form or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your event date, number of guests, chosen package, and any additional requirements or messages you share

If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we also receive whatever information you choose to share in that message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, when advertising or analytics are active we (and our partners) may automatically collect limited technical information such as your device type, browser, approximate location and pages viewed, using cookies and similar technologies. This helps us understand website traffic and measure our advertising.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and plan, quote and deliver catering for your event
  • To contact you about your booking (by phone, WhatsApp or email)
  • To improve our website and services
  • To measure and improve our advertising, and to show you relevant ads
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Cookies, analytics & advertising

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies from the following partners:

  • Google (Google Analytics and Google Ads) — to measure website traffic and the performance of our ads.
  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram Pixel) — to measure and optimise ads we run on Facebook and Instagram.

These partners may use cookies and device identifiers to show you ads based on your prior visits to our website or other sites. You can control or opt out of personalised advertising here:

  • Google Ads settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Google Analytics opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Meta ad preferences: facebook.com/adpreferences
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. How we store and share your information

Booking details submitted through our form are stored securely in Google Sheets (a Google service), accessible only to authorised members of Radha Madhav Caterers. We share information only as needed to run our business, for example:

  • With Google, which stores our booking data and provides analytics/advertising services.
  • With Meta, for advertising, where applicable.
  • Where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

6. Data retention

We keep your booking and enquiry details only for as long as needed to serve you and for our legitimate business records, after which we delete or anonymise them. You may ask us to delete your information sooner (see "Your rights" below).

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct any information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you or to use your data for marketing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.
